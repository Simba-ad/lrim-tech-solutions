Your website development fee is separate from any third-party services and optional ongoing Lrim-Tech support. We explain any required costs before your project begins so you can choose the setup that suits your business.

Website Care

  • Up to 2 small content changes per month

  • Major changes and new features quoted separately

Business Care

Other Costs You May Need

Domain Renewal Usually paid annually. The amount depends on the domain extension and registrar you choose.
Business Email May be free or paid depending on the email provider and features required.
Online Payments Payment gateways and transaction providers may charge their own setup, monthly or transaction fees.
Database / Backend Services Advanced websites and apps may need paid database, storage or server services as usage grows.
Third-Party Services & APIs Some integrations, messaging services, booking systems or external tools may have their own charges.
Major Changes & New Features New pages, redesigns, integrations and substantial functionality are quoted separately.
No surprise upgrades. If your website grows to the point where a free service is no longer sufficient, Lrim-Tech will explain the available options and costs before any paid upgrade is made.
